In progress · Bachelor's thesis

Self-optimising code vulnerability detection

A fine-tuned transformer classifier for function-level vulnerability detection, wrapped in a multi-agent optimisation loop. Three AI agents with separate roles train the model, evaluate it and debate the next hyperparameter configuration, iterating until the target metrics are met. The agentic loop is benchmarked against Optuna hyperparameter optimisation.

How it's built
  • Model foundation from my research internship at the University of Messina (2025): pre-trained CodeBERT and RoBERTa fine-tuned for binary vulnerability classification on the Big-Vul benchmark.
  • End-to-end pipeline: source preprocessing, tokenisation, class balancing, training, validation and held-out testing.
  • Evaluated on precision, recall, F1 and confusion matrices rather than accuracy alone, since vulnerable samples are the minority class.
  • Interactive dashboard: submit code, see the functions the model flags, and get AI-suggested fixes for each finding.

Not yet evaluated by my professors. Results will go here once they are.

In progress · Software engineering

Project Artha: autonomous trading system

A research trading system that forecasts market direction and treats loss minimisation as the primary objective: its risk layer is designed to cut losses quickly when a forecast is wrong. Engineered end to end to the formal software-engineering standards set by my professors, with a dashboard for monitoring the system live.

Why it's built this way
  • Developed as a software-engineering exercise: requirements, architecture, design and testing, following my professors' guidelines.
  • Risk management over prediction: success is measured by how well losses are contained, not only by forecast accuracy.
  • Monitoring dashboard for following the system's decisions and state.
  • Research only: no real capital is traded, and the code stays private until evaluation.

DEICMS: Digital Evidence & Integrity Management

A secure digital-forensics platform with a graph-based chain-of-custody engine that cryptographically verifies each evidence item's history and detects tampering.

Details
  • RBAC, TOTP two-factor authentication, rate limiting and an immutable audit trail.
  • SHA-256 integrity hashing, Ed25519 signatures and AES-256-GCM encryption at rest.
  • Isolation Forest anomaly detection feeding a multi-factor case risk score, plus a natural-language investigation assistant.
  • Hardened against CSRF, brute force, privilege escalation and malicious uploads.
